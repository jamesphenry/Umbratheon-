## 🔥 Narrative Origin

Cindervault wasn’t claimed—it was **revived**. Bombed into silence during the early fractures of galactic unrest, the crater-ridden planet became a perfect forge. Not because it was optimal—but because it was broken.

From fractured ground rose the Dominion’s fire-born automation. Nanite scaffolds clawed into old ruins. Solar veins were rekindled. And beneath the ash, production began. Endless. Tireless. Coded.

> The Dominion burns no fuel. It burns **purpose**.
