# Signal Interference Behavior

**Umbral Directive: SIG-4**  
*“We do not jam signals. We rewrite perception.”*

---

## 🧠 Purpose
Signal interference within the Umbral Dominion is not a matter of brute jamming. It is a psychological and informational weapon—used to manipulate timing, provoke misreads, and delay enemy cohesion.

> Disruption is not noise. It is suggestion.

---

## 📡 Interference Modes

### 1. **Echo Bleed**
- Sends phantom pings resembling ship movement
- Used to mask real fleet arrivals or departures
- Often triggered during outbound fleet decoys

### 2. **Loop Injection**
- Triggers previously captured scout data
- Replays clean conditions in a now-defended region
- Activated via lab-based feedback systems (see Fluxhold)

### 3. **Temporal Drift**
- Alters delay perception in enemy detection timers
- Stacks with cloaking effects to create false lulls
- Causes enemies to commit fleets into guarded space

---

## 🛰 Implementation Tactics
- **Relay Moons** like Fluxhold handle injection and echo patterning
- Bases like Glitchyard act as repeaters using short-range disruptors
- Strategic deployment of auto-scouts to feed falsified feedback loops

---

## 🔒 Operational Security
- Do not use interference in unguarded systems—it attracts scans
- Always reinforce affected regions with defense-ready nodes
- Avoid multiple patterns in a single region to prevent learning
