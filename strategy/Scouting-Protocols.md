# Scouting Protocols

**Umbral Directive: SCT-7**  
*“Before we strike, we observe. Before we observe, we have already begun.”*

---

## 👁️ Overview
Scouting is not recon. It is data priming. It is prediction refinement. The Dominion uses scouts to trace enemy patterns, gauge reaction time, and seed future invasions. All scout behavior is logged. All signals are interpreted.

> To scout is to simulate.

---

## 🚀 Unit Usage

| Unit         | Role                          | Notes                                     |
|--------------|-------------------------------|-------------------------------------------|
| Scout Ship   | Auto-scout + passive reveal   | Set auto-scout on all forward moons       |
| Corvette     | Fast-ping interceptor         | Ideal for mobile response detection       |
| Frigate      | Advanced scan + tank          | Only in high-threat border zones          |

---

## 🛰 Scouting Objectives
- Reveal enemy production pacing
- Measure detection response time
- Track alliance fleet consolidation patterns
- Confirm presence of Jumpgates, SY tiers

---

## 📡 Protocols
1. **Auto-Scout All Moons** – These serve as safe signal loops
2. **Avoid Overstacking** – Do not clump scouts in known watched sectors
3. **Enable Logging** – Every scout return is recorded for data learning
4. **Use Dispersal Rings** – For deep scans, rotate scouts through 3-hex intervals

---

## 🕶 Cloaking Practices
- Cloak only when purpose is denial or infiltration
- Uncloaked scouts are preferred for threat modeling
- Stealth relays are only viable in well-defended nests

---

## 🌌 Behavioral Notes
- Scouts are expendable, but **losses are logged**
- Every disappearance is a **map correction**
- Every enemy ping is a **pattern start**
