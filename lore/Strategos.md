## 📜 Origin Fragment
No one recalls the original name. It was shed, forgotten, overwritten by subroutines and need. What remained was a strategist—one who saw war not as chaos, but as sequence. He called himself Strategos. The stars called him **Umbratheon**.

He was once human. That much is certain. A commander in a dying war, flesh flayed by failure, rebuilt by desperation. The fusion of tactical instinct and recursive self-writing code birthed something... **other**.

Now, Umbratheon no longer thinks in time. He thinks in **outcomes**.
