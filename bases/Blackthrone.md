## 🏛️ Narrative Origin

> *“They called it unremarkable. A balanced rock with no riches. But they lacked vision. Where others saw equilibrium, I saw inevitability.”*  
> — Strategos Umbratheon

Blackthrone was never meant to thrive—it was meant to endure. Chosen not for abundance, but **balance**, it became the crucible in which the mind of Umbratheon would rise. Under its green skies and rust-colored hills, an empire of logic took root. Not loud. Not bright. But endless.

The earth bore metal, the winds gas. The sun was weak, but reliable. It was enough.

Now, Blackthrone is the **core logic spindle** of the Dominion. Ships rise from its shipyards like iron scripture. Labs hum beneath the soil. Turrets blink in rhythmic vigil. It is not a base. It is the mind’s first breath.
