## Chapter 0: The Signal

It began not with a war, but a **signal**—a whisper pulsed from the rim of a cratered world, looping endlessly in archaic code.

> _Coordinates decrypted. Thread unspooling... Identity hash confirmed. Subject: Strategos Umbratheon. Status: Awakening._

Cindervault was the first to respond. Born from scorched stone and silence, it rose—a vault not for the dead, but for the reborn. Steel limbs dragged alloys into shape. Drones hummed. And the stars... the stars began listening.
