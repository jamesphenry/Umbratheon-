# Threat Assessment

**Umbral Directive: THR-8**  
*“Threat is not presence. It is potential.”*

---

## 🔍 Overview
Threat levels are assigned not by size, but by unpredictability, velocity, and alignment deviation. A fleet is not dangerous because it exists—but because of **where it moves**, and **what it avoids**.

All threat ratings are internal. Diplomacy does not affect analysis.

---

## 🧪 Threat Evaluation Criteria

| Factor         | Weight   | Notes                                                      |
|----------------|----------|------------------------------------------------------------|
| Fleet Velocity | High     | Jump paths, return speed, idle gaps                        |
| Cloak Patterns | High     | Repeated stealth near shared zones                         |
| Tech Gaps      | Medium   | Unusual spike in armor/weapon without known source         |
| Communication  | Medium   | Silent actors with sudden contact                          |
| Redacted Logs  | Medium   | Removal of data from guild logs, especially fleet edits    |
| Location Drift | High     | Bases shifting without conflict trigger                    |

---

## ⚠️ Behavior Flags

- **Silent Clustering**: Multiple fleets with no recent activity near wormholes
- **Interleaved Timers**: Arrival windows precisely 1–3 minutes apart (attack cue)
- **Cross-Sector Spikes**: Bases reporting multiple small pings from separate sectors
- **Fleet Edits > 3x in 24h**: Indicates planning or manipulation
