# Economic Constructs

**Umbral Directive: ECO-1**  
*“Credits are not currency. They are combustion.”*

---

## 💰 Overview
Economic development under the Umbral Dominion is a tool of expansion, not luxury. Credits fuel fleet growth, construction acceleration, and infrastructure layering. Every structure must return on its cost.

---

## 🧾 Core Credit Sources

### 🏙 Urban Structures
- Enable higher population → drives base scaling
- Unlocks structure cap; always optimized first

### 🏦 Economic Centers
- Best passive income per slot
- Scale slowly—only when stable income needed
- Ideal Levels: 5–10 for support bases, 15+ for anchor worlds

### 💻 Android + Nanite Factories
- Reduce time → indirectly increases economic throughput
- High Android levels (Lvl 5+) best on economic cores

### 🔬 Research Labs
- Unlock passive tech bonuses that reduce costs across all ops
- Pair with economic builds on moons or Earths

---

## 🛠 Efficiency Tactics
- **Delay Eco Centers** until refineries, shipyards, and ports are sustainable
- **Never go negative energy**—energy penalty kills economic output
- **Balance Androids and Labs** to reduce tax impact and boost returns
- Use high-fertility planets sparingly for eco builds unless long-term anchor

---

## 🌐 Interplanetary Strategy
- Link economic nodes to supply fleets and core forges (e.g. Cindervault)
- Never cluster multiple eco bases in same vulnerable region
- Use Dreadcore-style interceptors to protect wealth hubs
