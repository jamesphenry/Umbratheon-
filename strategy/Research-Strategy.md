# Research Strategy

**Umbral Directive: RS-0**  
*“Knowledge is not power. It is precision.”*

---

## 🧬 Overview
The Dominion’s research philosophy prioritizes **return-on-tech** and tactical advantages over scientific prestige. Every tech investment must pay dividends in construction speed, unit power, or economic efficiency.

> The lab is not a temple. It is a forge.

---

## 🧠 Tech Tier Priorities

### 🥇 Tier 1: Foundational
- **Construction** → unlocks factories, nanites
- **Energy** → expands power plants (Solar/Fusion/AM)
- **Computer** → research rate and fleet control
- **Weapons/Armor/Shields** → enable unit upgrades

### 🥈 Tier 2: Expansion Techs
- **Advanced Ship Designs** (Cruisers, Carriers, Frigates)
- **Android Technology** → automated production
- **Jumpgates** (later game only)
- **Terraforming** → conditional, based on area

### 🥉 Tier 3: Strategic Diversifiers
- **Cloaking** → only for intel-focused bases
- **Plasma/Disruptors** → late game only
- **Ion Technology** → situational; minor gains

---

## 🧪 Base Strategy Alignment
- **Blackthrone**: Research hub, 20+ Labs standard
- **Fluxhold**: Stealth + data-focused science (14 Labs)
- **Other Bases**: Minimal labs unless long-term or moon-based

---

## 🧰 Optimization Notes
- Always match **Labs** with sufficient **Urban** and **Energy**
- Avoid over-investing in Labs without Androids (waste of time)
- Labs >15 only worthwhile on Moons or Core planets
