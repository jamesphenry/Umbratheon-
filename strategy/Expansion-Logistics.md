# Expansion Logistics

**Umbral Directive: EXL-2**  
*“Territory is not taken. It is aligned.”*

---

## 📦 Expansion Philosophy
Growth within the Dominion is not measured in conquest but in **functionality**. A new base must fill a role—production, research, defense, or visibility. Random expansion dilutes strength. Planned expansion defines inevitability.

---

## 📍 Core Expansion Types

### 1. **Anchor Nodes**
- High area planets with balanced resources
- Long-term development cores (e.g. Blackthrone)
- Require high Urban, Nanite, Economic scaling

### 2. **Utility Outposts**
- Asteroids or Moons near trade/fleet routes
- Specialize in fleet storage, interception, logistics
- Easy to harden, easy to abandon

### 3. **Signal Forges**
- Cratered planets or industrial moons
- Purpose-built constructors and supply centers (e.g. Cindervault)
- Lower defense; prioritize redundancy

### 4. **Relay Shadows**
- Intelligence / scout nests
- Often Moons or Asteroids
- Labs + Cloak, low fleet profile, high detection reach (see Fluxhold)

---

## 🔄 Expansion Protocol
1. **Secure Fleet Superiority** in nearby hex before construction
2. **Deploy Outpost/Colony Ship** to validated astrotype
3. **Redact Base Location** from shared data if role is critical
4. **Begin Function-Specific Build Queue** within 30 mins

---

## 🚨 Risk Flags
- Never cluster more than 2 core-value bases per system
- Avoid crystal-heavy areas unless targeting research-only roles
- Build multiple fallback spots for each core planet
