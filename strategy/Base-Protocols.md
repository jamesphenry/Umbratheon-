**Umbral Dominion Directive: BIP-0**  
*“A structure is not a building. It is a function, frozen in alloy.”*

---

## 🏗️ Overview
This document outlines the Dominion's foundational principles for planetary development across different astrotypes. Efficiency, resilience, and interconnectivity are prioritized above comfort or aesthetics. Bases are not homes—they are **nodes**.

---

## 🧱 Core Construction Sequence
Applicable to most bases (Moon, Planet, Asteroid):

1. **Urban Structures** – Always expanded early; caps everything else.
2. **Solar/Gas Plants** – Based on solar value; aim for flexible energy base.
3. **Metal Refineries** – First resource to scale; sets early fleet and building speed.
4. **Robotic Factories** – Drives build time reduction.
5. **Shipyards + Spaceports** – Add when fleet or logistics is a focus.
6. **Nanite Factories** – Minimum Lvl 5 for all permanent bases.
7. **Android Factories** – Lvl 3–5 depending on need; delay if low area.
8. **Fusion/AM Plants** – Only when Solar hits diminishing returns.
9. **Economic Centers** – Added as base income or endgame ramp-up.
10. **Terraform** – Only used if Area < 70 and base is non-temporary.

## 🪐 Astrotype-Specific Notes

### Earth-like Planets
- Balanced builds
- Excellent for research or hybrid production
- Expand energy slowly (Solar+Fusion mix)

### Cratered Planets
- High area, decent fertility
- Ideal for Nanite-heavy forges
- Defense-first design (see Cindervault)

### Moons
- Limited area, better for intel, storage, or fleet nesting
- High lab viability due to low defense profile (see Fluxhold)

### Asteroids
- Low fertility, small area
- Specialize quickly: scramble node, defense bastion, or refit point

---

## 🧠 Dominion Guidelines
- Never overinvest in Crystals unless strategic tech demands it.
- Jumpgates should only be placed at highly connected spines.
- Do not exceed Lvl 10 Androids unless at Blackthrone or deep R&D node.
- Orbital Shipyards require strong energy backing; avoid before Fusion/AM base is stable.
