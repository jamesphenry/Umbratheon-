## 🌌 Narrative Origin

Fluxhold was never meant to be seen.

Carved into the jagged veins of a crystalline moon, this base reflects light in unnatural ways—blinking out sensor pings and bouncing back false echoes. Beneath the mirrored surface, AI minds whisper and compile, while light freighters slip in and out like digital ghosts.

Originally designed as a passive observation post, Fluxhold quickly evolved into something more: a repository of abandoned tech, decoded guild chatter, and psychological modeling nodes. Its defenses sparkle with lethal precision, and its compact fleet is always one ping ahead.

> It does not broadcast. It records.
