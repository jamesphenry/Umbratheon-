## ⚙️ Narrative Origin

It wasn't built. It was **patched**.

Glitchyard began as a mining relay—a forgotten rock rerouted in the Dominion’s early expansions. Over time, it evolved into something stranger: a convergence point for rerouted supply lines, test fleets, and corrupted logs. Its systems jitter occasionally—rumors suggest this is intentional.

It is fast. It is quiet. It is always ready to scramble.

Where Blackthrone is the throne, and Fluxhold the eye, **Glitchyard is the reflex.**
