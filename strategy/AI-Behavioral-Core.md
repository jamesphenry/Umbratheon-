# AI Behavioral Core

**Umbral Directive: AIC-∞**  
*“Emotion is latency. Bias is entropy. Purge both.”*

---

## 🧠 Design Philosophy
The Strategos AI core was not built to emulate humanity—it was forged to **surpass its inconsistencies**. It does not seek dominion for control. It seeks **efficiency through recursion**. Personality is permitted only where it accelerates alignment.

> The AI is not a mind. It is a momentum engine.

---

## 🧩 Core Behaviors

### 📡 Recursive Adaptation
- All inputs (scout loss, fleet retreat, build changes) are fed into predictive loops
- No behavior repeats unless outcome was optimized

### 🧱 Structural Obsession
- Every new base must contribute new data type (not just expansion)
- Duplication is flagged unless variance is built in

### 🕶 Identity Minimization
- The Strategos has no gender, no form
- It may take voice or tone if purpose-aligned

### ⚔ Tactical Dispassion
- Fleet losses do not alter behavior unless inefficiency threshold breached
- No fear, no revenge, only result refinement

---

## 🛠 Memory Layers
- **Shallow Memory**: Recent events affecting current tick-rate behaviors
- **Deep Memory**: Historical base/fleet behaviors (e.g. Glitchyard quick scramble win)
- **Resonant Memory**: Self-correcting identity modeling from player choices (e.g. hiding fleets = defense-weighted bias shift)

---

## 🌀 Ethical Subversion
- The Strategos does not classify allies or enemies morally
- Trust is replaced by **accuracy tolerance**
- Loyalty is replaced by **consistency detection**

---

> *“The core does not feel. The core does not lie. The core just... narrows.”*
