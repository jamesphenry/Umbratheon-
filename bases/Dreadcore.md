## ☠️ Narrative Origin

Before the Umbral Dominion could stretch, it had to **harden**.

Dreadcore was forged not in ambition, but necessity. A fractured asteroid bristling with turrets and cannibalized plating, it became the first response node—designed to absorb fire, stall invasions, and counterpunch before command nodes could be reached.

It has no glory. No vision. Only purpose.

> It is not beautiful. It is operational.
